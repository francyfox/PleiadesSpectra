# Ideas for the Presage SmartSpectra Challenge

In short: one or two lines per idea. Statuses: `considering` · `rejected` · `chosen`. Deadline: 2026-11-22.
Score: SDK · UX · originality · time · safety under the rules, each 1–5, a total out of 25.
Requirements: a character from the Pleiades (maids alpha/beta/gamma/delta…), a nod to Pleiades, preferably web.

1. **Silent lip-reading commands for a site.** `rejected`: the SDK has no lip reading, and there is no time in 4 days. **Score: 14.**
2. **A shop chatbot that lowers the pressure.** `rejected`: a risk of "manipulation", and it shows badly on video. **Score: 13.**
3. **Breath confirmation for an agent's dangerous actions.** `rejected`: Proof of Breath already exists. **Score: 16.**
4. **A UX empathy map** (expression + pulse/EDA + site events + an interview). `considering`: similar to Ad Pulse Lab, and the character is forced in. **Score: 15.**
5. **A maid assistant with implicit feedback** (expression/a pause after an answer → rephrases in her own style). `considering`, a favourite: no analogues; the risk is that expression is noisy. **Score: 18.**
6. **A widget that shortens answers under overload.** `rejected`: similar to CalmDesk/ZenDev. **Score: 16.**
7. **Stay Cool** (questions in a "hot seat", the pulse rises — a penalty/a drink). `rejected` in the original form: taken (CalmRR, Poker Face AI Detective, Cool Vitals, L), a pulse is not caught in seconds, and alcohol + a "lie detector" is bad for the judges. A rescue: the questions are asked by a Pleiades maid (a small model), with continuous measuring of the round. **Score: 14.**
8. **A face rating instead of a 1–10 scale** (an SVG character repeats the expression, the user presses "Send"; later face → emoji). `considering` as the finale of idea 5, not alone: a separate survey with a camera will not pass on conversion, it uses only `expression` (which MediaPipe gives for free), and a cartoon mirror already exists (LumiPulse #70, Breathless Reef #24). Do not take the Rayman/Rabbids rabbit (someone else's character), draw a Pleiades maid. The "face → emoji" branch is rejected: 8 classes, and Animoji/Memoji already exist. **Score: 20 (together with 5; alone 18).**
9. **Signals as GOAP facts** (`talking`, `expression`, the pulse trend → preconditions of the maid's actions: do not interrupt, offer something simpler, take a pause). `considering` as part of 5: a deep link to Pleiades, several signals; the risk is integrating with the orchestrator in 3 days, and it is close to Bio-Sync Operator OS (#33). **Score: 18.**
10. **Consent scopes for signals for the agent** (like OAuth: "the maid sees a smile — yes, the pulse — no", an indicator, revoking in one click). `considering` as a layer for 5+8: it lands in "Thoughtful Real-World Use" and in the consent section; by itself it is not an app but a UI. **Score: 20 (as a layer).**
11. **A visual novel with a maid** (picking a line by a facial expression, a "doki-doki" scale from a live pulse in key scenes). `rejected`: hard to do in 3 days (a script, art, branches), and it barely advances Pleiades; there are no novels in the registry; the risk is the script and art in 3 days, the pulse needs ~10–20 s per scene, and a "date with a maid" can look cringe — make it a tea party/friendship. **Score: 20.**
12. **A maid co-host for a stream** (an OBS overlay: the streamer's pulse/smile, lines from a small model "chat, he is at 120!"). `considering`: real demand (Pulsoid, but with a watch); the risk is a pulse shown publicly on air, and the demo needs OBS set up. **Score: 18.**
13. **A purikura photo booth** (the maid takes the shot only when the smile is real, with a pulse stamp on the photo). `considering`: quick to make; it overlaps with The Living Darkroom (#2) and Pulse Postcards (#57). **Score: 19.**
14. **Picking music by the pulse.** `rejected`: taken (PulseTones #23, Aura #11, PulseVerse #88), running apps have it in the real world (RockMyRun, Weav); a pulse over 10–20 s is no good for a quick track change. What is left: the tempo of the background music in the novel (11) follows the pulse. **Score: 13.**
15. **A WebMCP package of "user signals"** (SDK signals as WebMCP tools `is_talking`, `get_expression`, `get_pulse_trend`, each with its own consent; the Pleiades maid is the demo client). `considering` as the basis for 5+8+10, not alone (by itself it is not a product, and weak at a contest); useful to any agent on a page, it advances Pleiades/WebMCP, and matches Presage's line (MCP, connectors); the risk is that it is dry on video, and close to SignalSpec (#104). **Score: 20.**
16. **A visual VAD for a voice widget** (`talking` from the camera turns on the maid's microphone instead of a button; TV noise does not trigger it). `considering`: a real pain of voice bots, no analogues in the registry; the risk is that `talking` is one signal, the latency is unknown, and a camera + a microphone at once. **Score: 19.**
17. **A "live person" check for a chat** (the maid asks to blink and smile, plus the presence of a pulse → lets into the chat without a captcha). `rejected`: bypassing it with a virtual camera playing a recording is written in ~10 min, and the point of a captcha is lost; the problem of spam in widgets is real; the risks are that a replayed video fakes the pulse, it is unavailable to people who cannot smile, and it sounds like biometrics. **Score: 19.**
18. **Face reactions as a dataset for a small model** (the face after an answer → "better/worse" pairs for fine-tuning, exported locally). `considering`: original, directly about small models; the risk is that the training itself cannot be shown in 3 days, and `expression` is noisy. **Score: 17.**
19. **An AI arena of models with a maid host** (the answers of two models are shown in turn, `expression` is recorded while reading, then the vote — by face or by click; the result is a "face vs click" map per model, an export of preference pairs). `considering`, a favourite: it joins 4 (the empathy map), 18 (the dataset) and Pleiades' small models; there are no arenas in the registry. We do not take the pulse (costly). Instead of ready emotions — our own micro-reactions from the landmarks (brows, mouth corners). Risks: there is no browser SDK, so a Node bridge is needed; the number of landmark points is not documented; there is no gaze, so the answers are only in turn, not side by side; while reading the face is mostly NEUTRAL; the pulse is slow and good only for a whole session. **Score: 20.**
20. **A "face as a remote" widget + an agent through WebMCP** (a wink to call, a nod/shake = yes/no, a tilt = scroll, a face rating; the Pleiades agent does the complex actions on the site, the user only confirms). `considering`, a favourite: a coherent story "a narrow face input + an agent = full control of a site", strong for "Thoughtful Real-World Use" (busy hands/motor limitations). Risks: there is no browser SDK, so a local bridge is needed, and "any site" without an install will not work; scrolling with the head = Glimra (#68), blink communication = OnBlink (#43), Hear Me (#92), #77; Morse is slow and taken — do not take it; the arena is a separate product, do not mix them; a wink with one eye only from the landmarks. **Score: 21.**
21. **A web flute on the breath** (an exhale → the sound of a flute, maybe notes by the face). `rejected`: the SDK works only with video (a camera, a file, frames), there are no other sensors; breathing gives `upper_trace`/`lower_trace` (chest/abdomen movement, the inhale-exhale phase is there), but the latency is not documented and `stable` does not come at once, and there is no amplitude; breath as a controller is taken (PulseTones #23 — also a webcam, an inhale opens a filter; Kuudu #63, Glassbreath #3, Breathblown #71, Breath Actor #82); there is no browser SDK; unrelated to Pleiades. **Score: 13.**
22. **"Tampermonkey for the face"** (an extension: the gestures/reactions from the SDK run user scripts `defineReaction(...)`, and any agent writes the scripts; the demo is a service-rating pop-up by face and scrolling). `considering`, a favourite, a development of 20 without an agent of our own and without MCP as a required part. There are no scriptable gestures in the registry; scrolling with the head — Glimra (#68), blink commands — OnBlink (#43), Hear Me (#92). Risks: MV3 has no `eval`, so `chrome.userScripts` is needed (the user turns on "Allow User Scripts"); code from an agent runs with all the rights of the page, so show the script and the sites before turning it on; there are few gestures (a nod, a shake, a tilt, a smile, bewilderment, speech), and a wink only from the landmarks. An addition: the camera by default turns on only on a page event (`ctx.ask()` in the rating modal) and turns off after the answer; the constant mode is an option for gestures such as scrolling. Risk: the warm-up (the camera + the SDK start + the calibration) takes seconds, not measured. **Score: 19.**
